Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

RoleReady AI ("RoleReady", "we", "our") provides AI-assisted resume, job-application and interview preparation services. This policy explains how we handle information.

Information we process

We process information you provide, including account email and authentication information, uploaded resumes, job descriptions, work history, application materials, generated documents, and saved draft metadata. When you submit feedback, we may store your comments, rating, feedback category, optional email and application version. Hosting and authentication providers may process technical information required to deliver and secure the service.

AI features

When you use AI-assisted features, relevant inputs may be sent to OpenAI to produce results. Do not submit sensitive information that is unnecessary for the task. We do not guarantee that AI output is correct. OpenAI's own terms and policies also govern its processing.

Cloud drafts and files

If you use cloud-saving features, RoleReady AI stores application drafts and associated data in Supabase. This can include job descriptions, account email, original resumes and generated resume files. Data you save may persist across sessions. Not all content you enter is necessarily saved as a cloud draft.

How information is used

We use information to provide requested features, authenticate users, maintain saved drafts, manage customer support and subscriptions, troubleshoot errors, improve the service, prevent abuse and comply with law. We do not offer uploaded resumes for sale to recruiters or advertisers as part of the intended service.

Service providers

Our service uses OpenAI (AI processing), Supabase (cloud storage and database), Streamlit-hosted infrastructure (application delivery), Google (where Google sign-in is used) and Stripe (where paid billing is enabled). These providers may process data as necessary to perform their services, subject to their respective terms.

Payments

Stripe handles payment processing for paid subscriptions. RoleReady AI may receive billing identifiers, transaction records and subscription status needed to administer access and support. We do not intend to collect full payment-card numbers through our own application forms.

Retention and deletion

Saved drafts may be retained according to available retention settings or until deleted. The application includes a draft-deletion function that attempts to remove the draft record and associated stored resume files. File removal may fail independently, and provider backups or required records may persist for additional periods. We therefore cannot promise immediate deletion of every copy. To request broader deletion or access to personal data, contact the support address below. We will assess requests under applicable law.

Security

We use service-provider infrastructure and application safeguards intended to protect data. No online service can guarantee complete security. Do not upload information that is unnecessary for career-related features.

Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to submit optional feedback or use cloud-saving features. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Contact us to submit a request.

Children

RoleReady AI is designed for adult career users and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date shown above when we do.
